How it works

Shoot, check, pick

It works on each shot just after it is taken. Live-view guidance is not available yet.

  1. Your tethering software saves each shot

    AI Curator reads new JPG files in that folder on the studio PC. We'll confirm which camera and software combinations are supported when we talk.

  2. Each shot is checked

    Closed eyes, expression, cropped heads, shoulders or feet, the number of people, blur, exposure, and whether a pet has left the frame.

  3. A short tip for the next shot

    One sentence on screen and by voice.

  4. The customer picks

    It suggests up to 12 shots that came out well. The customer makes the final pick.

For Korean passport and ID photos, it checks the Ministry of Foreign Affairs rules and, when staff ask, saves a cropped copy as a new file. The original stays untouched. It does not guarantee acceptance. Other countries' photo rules are not supported.

Principles

Ground rules

It does not edit photos

No retouching, filters, background or face swaps. Original files are never changed or deleted.

It does not identify people

No face recognition and no face templates. It checks the shot, not the person.

Analysis stays on the studio PC

Photos are checked in the browser on the studio PC. They are not sent to a server or used for AI training.

Customers know it's AI, and can switch it off

Customers are told up front that the tips come from AI, and can turn them off with one button (labelled in Korean in the prototype). Privacy and AI rules differ by country, so before use we go through the customer notice and privacy wording with each studio. This is not legal advice, and in some places we cannot offer it yet.

Original Data proof

Register the originals you hand over

Studios will be able to register the original files they give to customers. The record keeps each file's fingerprint (SHA-256 hash), which studio registered it, and when. Anyone can then check whether a file they have is byte-for-byte the same as the registered one. DataPD does not receive the file.

It does not check whether the content is true, whether it was made with AI, who first created it, copyright, or legal effect.
